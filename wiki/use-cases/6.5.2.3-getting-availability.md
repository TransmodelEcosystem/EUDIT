# 6.5.2.3 Getting availability with or without reservation

## Summary
When choosing a TRAVEL PACKAGE for a specific trip, with or without making 
a reservation, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER wants to be informed about the 
availabilities of each solution and their options before making a purchase.
The customer may or may not place a reservation, temporarily or confirmed. An 
availability check may be done in advance to preplan (e.g., to check availability
of product or seat) or also at the time of travel to check the current availability 
of assets.

## Steps

## Description

It is assumed that possible trip options (trip patterns) are provided by a trip 
planner and that the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER wants to know about the 
different solutions composed with different SALES OFFER PACKAGEs (in 
particular, prices). 
One trip proposal is sent to the fare calculators. Solutions are returned, each
expressed by a TRAVEL PACKAGE (based on relevant SALES OFFER 
PACKAGEs). They can be several on each trip proposal. A TRAVEL PACKAGE is 
composed with zero, one or more FARE PRODUCTs on each leg or is combining 
many legs.
If the product has no mandatory reservation nor options that need separate 
pricing, the final price is given by the fare calculator, and the TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMER can directly select and buy this FARE PRODUCT.
If a FARE PRODUCT has mandatory reservation and/or options that need 
pricing, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must select every needed data: class, 
deck, window or corridor, bike space, etc.. The platform starts the reservation 
process to block the selected options until the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER
purchases it (or abandons it or after a limited period). It is the pre-purchase 
step.
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can modify/add ancillaries/options at this stage.
If there is an issue with the reservation (example: another TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMER has purchased the last seat in the meantime), the TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMER must be alerted in order to modify a parameter and thus the
TRAVEL PACKAGE.
Which parameters and options are mandatory depends on the product. For a 
point-to-point fare, they include the specific origin and destination of the trip
(with scheduled timing). Further parameters may be needed to fully specify
access rights, such as the selection of a seat reservation, travel class, luggage 
space and onboard services. For pass products allowing repeated travel, they 
might include a zone or an origin-destination pair, and a time period. 
For all the FARE PRODUCTs, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER is informed about
the travel conditions and related TRAVEL GUARANTEEs. 
Once any mandatory reservation and other options are selected, the 
TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can proceed to the purchase and travel.
