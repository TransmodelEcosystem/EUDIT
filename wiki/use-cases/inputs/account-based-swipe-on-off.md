#### Account-based swipe-on/swipe-off with on-demand inspection QR

The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER uses a MaaS app as a MEDIUM ACCESS DEVICE hosting a MEDIUM APPLICATION INSTANCE for account-based ticketing. They swipe on as a bus arrives, triggering a PASSENGER CHECK IN EVENT against their FARE CONTRACT. The journey is recorded on the back-end without a pre-issued paper ticket. When a ticket inspector boards the bus, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER requests an on-demand QR code; the app fetches a short-lived, cryptographically signed TRAVEL DOCUMENT token; the inspector scans it and the CONTROL PASSENGER TRIP EVENT is resolved. At destination the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER swipes off, finalising the FARE CONTRACT ENTRY and the fare debit. 

**Steps:**
1. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER opens the MaaS app; FARE CONTRACT is active and linked to their account via the MEDIUM APPLICATION INSTANCE; account balance or pass entitlement is sufficient for the journey
2. Bus arrives; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER taps "Swipe on" in the app; a PASSENGER CHECK IN EVENT is recorded against the active FARE CONTRACT, capturing the stop identifier, service reference, and timestamp; a FARE TRIP ACTIVATION ENTRY is created on the back-end, posting a tentative maximum-fare hold on the account
3. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER boards the bus; back-end registers the trip via an ACCOUNT DETECT TRIP EVENT and associates it with the PASSENGER CHECK IN ENTRY; no physical ticket is issued at this point
4. Mid-journey a ticket inspector boards the bus; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER taps "Show QR" in the app
5. MEDIUM APPLICATION INSTANCE requests an on-demand TRAVEL DOCUMENT token from the back-end (BoB token endpoint); back-end issues a short-lived, cryptographically signed token embedding the FARE CONTRACT reference, service and operator identifiers, boarding stop, timestamp, and a narrow validity window (e.g. 30 seconds); the QR code is rendered on-screen
6. Inspector scans the QR with their CONTROL MEANS device; a CONTROL PASSENGER TRIP EVENT is triggered; the device verifies the cryptographic signature, checks the token against the WHITELIST / BLACKLIST via a PASSENGER SECURITY LIST CHECK EVENT, and confirms the access rights are valid for this service
7. If the check passes, inspector acknowledges; a CONTROL PASSENGER TRIP ENTRY is logged against the FARE CONTRACT; if the check fails (expired token, blacklisted account, insufficient rights) an INSUFFICIENT ACCESS RIGHTS ENTRY is recorded and the inspector follows the revenue-protection procedure
8. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER arrives at destination and swipes off; a PASSENGER CHECK OUT EVENT is recorded; back-end computes the actual fare from the check-in/check-out pair, releases the maximum-fare hold, posts the final debit as a FARE CONTRACT ENTRY, and sends a receipt to the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER
