**Business trip in a big city**
--
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has a meeting in the other side of a big city. 

Steps :
1. At his office, he TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks at the city map (with metro and tramway stations) and FARE PRODUCTs available for his trip, knowing that he probably has to do this trip again 2 or 3 time during next years. He doesn't know if each time it be him or his colleague. 
2. At the metro station, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchase on an automatic vending machine an anonymous contactless card with 10 tokens. Each token is valid for metro and tram with connexions for a duration of 1h30 at maximum. The card is valid for many years and can be reloaded with 10 tokens each time. 
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER pays the purchase with his professional credit card.
4. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER takes the metro and then has a transfer with a tramway. One token is consumed. The travel takes 1 hour. 
5. For the return trip, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER uses again his card and consumes another token. 
6. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can be inspected : he shows the card to the inspector that checks the dates and hours of the validations.
