**Pass paid by third parties**

1. A contract is established between the CUSTOMER, the operator and the third parties. (school, university, company).
2. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchases a transport card with a monthly pass (monthly paid at the end od month) 
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER gives the card ID to his company. 
4. Each month, the enterprise send a list of TRAVEL DOCUMENTs (ID) with an amount of participation (called PDE in France; Company Travel Plan). Example : 20€ on each employee and 30€ for the sales staff. It may also be the departement providing a contribution for student cards. A file is exchanged each month. 
5. Each month, the operator makes a sale by TRAVEL DCOUMENT and checks that the contract between parties is valid. The sale is paid by several third parties and the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER. 

**Different VAT rates** 

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER is on a long-distance train. The train operator offers the possibility of purchasing  one or more metro or tramway single-trip tickets during the train journey. The ticket is sold by train operator with fees subject to 20% VAT and the ticket fare subject to 10% VAT.
2. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchases 10 tickets using a bank card. He receives a receipt with detailed information on the fees and transport ticket prices. 

 **Different prices**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER connects to a website which compares transport ticket prices. This web site can display different prices for the same ticket using different RETAILERs.
2. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER browses the displayed solutions (different guarantees, after-sales options) and chooses the best price. 
3. On the same website, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchases the transport ticket he chose at the selected price. 

 **Many FARE PRODUCTS at the same time**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has to manage the daily transport mobility cards for the whole family : adults going to work and children going to school. He does not wants to go to an agency : he manages this from home.
2. Every September, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has to connect to the city mobility website to purchase cards and passes for the year. 
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can select a new contactless card with a scholar yearly pass onto it, a yearly student pass on an existing card and two adult zonal passes on existing cards. 
4. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER selects the required FARE PRODUCTs, provides the details for scholar/student profile and reductions.
5. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER pays in one transaction. 
6. The new card is sent by post (with the pass encoded) and the others pass renewals are loaded onto the cards on the next first tap.

 **Newcomer in the city**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER knows which tickets he wants : he goes to a ticket vending machine. 
2. He selects a contactless ticket and also selects also 10 tokens to load onto it. 
3. He pays by bank card in one transaction.

 **SMS-ticket**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has a smartphone with ability to send and receive SMS messages (contract with phone operator).  
2. The city bus is arriving (in less than 5 minutes). The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER send an SMS message "CITYTCIKET" to the phone number 932012. 
3. The retailer receives the message, requests details linked to the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER's phone number from the phone operator and begins a ticket purchase (only one SALES OFFER PACKAGE available by message). 
4. The retailer requests the phone operator for the PRICE payment and if it is valid, ends the purchase. 
5. An SMS message is sent to the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER phone with the ticket details (ticket ID, FARE PRODUCT name, price, end of validity date and hour) 
6. For the inspection, an internet link (very short) to the receipt is given in the SMS : either the CUSTOMER or the inspector can load and consult the receipt (.pdf file).  

 **Reduction card**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has no reduction card. He decides to purchase one.
2. In the same payment, the TARNSPORt CUSTOMER wants a monthly PASS to paid at the price calculated with the reduciton card.
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER pays an amount composed with reduction card price and pass reduced price

 **Reimboursment and purchase in same payment**

1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has brought a product A last week but it is the wrong product for what he wants to do. He conects to his customer account on his applciation.
2. He requests for the reimboursment of the product A as it is allowed in the aftersales condition (not used, not stated).
3. He wants with this amount buy the new product B more adapted to his needs. So ha adds the pass B in his basket abd pays only the difference between the 2 prices (if product B is with higer price).
