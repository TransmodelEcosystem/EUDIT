**Prepare my holidays**
--
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER schedules a week's holiday with his family in another country. He wants to purchase in advance several independent tickets, for mobility and for visits. He has already his holiday accommodation. He wants to reserve and purchase transports tickets and museum visits. He needs for the first day one rail ticket and one museum entry for his family. 

Steps : 
1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks for museum entrance on the institutional city website : He chooses a visit time slot and adds to cart the tickets for the whole family. He does not pay but the visits are pre-reserved for 15 minutes (locked).
2. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks for the transport tickets according to the visits : he selects the origin zone and the destination zone for the rail ticket, he selects the JOURNEY in order to arrive on time for the entrance of the museum. 
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER enters the data required for the rail tickets : names and dates of birth. The price for a family ticket is displayed as the best offer. The travellers must show their identity card during inspection. He validates the ticket and adds it to his website cart. He does not pay. 
4.  The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER checks the entire schedule of this day in order to have enough time between the visit and transport. He finally adds an insurance add-on for the return JOURNEY by rail : ticket exchange without fees. 
5. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER checks the cart and the items inside : he validates and pays by credit card. 
6. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives the tickets by e-mail at home. He decides to print them.
