#### Shared scooter rental with pre-ride instruction confirmation, live zone overlay, and photo proof of parking

The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER books a shared scooter. Before the scooter is unlocked, mandatory pre-ride instructions — safety rules, helmet requirements, zone conditions — are presented and must be explicitly confirmed; the acknowledgement is recorded as a SALES NOTICE ASSIGNMENT on the booking. During the ride the app displays a live overlay of MOBILITY SERVICE CONSTRAINT ZONEs (no-parking, low-speed, no-access, school zones), which the operator can update in real time. At the end of the trip the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must photograph the parked scooter as geo-stamped proof of correct parking before the leg is formally closed.

**Steps:**
1. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER searches for and books a nearby shared scooter; SALES OFFER PACKAGE is confirmed and the scooter is assigned
2. Before unlock, app presents mandatory pre-ride instructions drawn from the SALES NOTICE ASSIGNMENT attached to the booking: legal requirements (helmet, footpath rules), operator conditions of use, and a summary of active MOBILITY SERVICE CONSTRAINT ZONEs in the riding area; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must confirm each item individually — acknowledgement is timestamped and stored against the booking
3. Only after full confirmation does the system send the unlock command (`{assetOperation}-asset/execution` unlock); if confirmation is not completed within a timeout the booking is released
4. During the ride the app displays a live zone overlay retrieved from the operator's zone feed: MOBILITY SERVICE CONSTRAINT ZONEs classified by TYPE OF ZONE RESTRICTION — no-parking zones, designated parking areas, low-speed corridors, no-access zones, and school zones (with active time windows); each zone boundary is rendered with its applicable VEHICLE TYPE ZONE RESTRICTION (e.g. scooters only, all micro-mobility)
5. Operator can push real-time zone updates at any time during the ride (new temporary restrictions, event closures, dynamic school-zone activation); app refreshes the overlay and alerts the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER if their current position enters a newly restricted zone
6. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER approaches end of trip; app checks the current GPS position against the zone feed and confirms whether the intended parking spot falls within a designated parking area; if not, the app highlights the nearest valid zone
7. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER parks the scooter within a valid zone; app requires a geo- and time-stamped photo of the parked vehicle before the leg can be ended; photo must show the scooter and a recognisable landmark or marking
8. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER submits the photo; system attaches it to the leg-end event and ends the leg (`{legOperation}-leg/execution` end with photo payload and GPS coordinates); PARKING BAY CONDITION at the drop-off point is updated
9. System validates the parking position against the zone feed (automated geo-check) and, where applicable, queues the photo for operator spot-check; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives a receipt with the final fare and a parking confirmation reference
