**Billing at the end of the month at the best fare**
--
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER subscribe to a mobility service and pays at the end of the month for the travels he did. The TRAVELER goes every day to his work company and during week-ends, he goes to cinema and for shopping in the city-center. 

Steps : 
1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks for a FARE PRODUCT that allows him to pay only when he uses public transport. He subscribes to a SALES OFFER PACKAGES and gives a payment method for the billing. He subscribe for a year, automatically renewed until he manually ends the subscription. The allowed zones are different between week and week-end : with one trip between zone 2 and zone 1 for his job and all zones for the weekend. 
3.  The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER travels all the month on the transport networks that accept this FARE CONTRACT.
4.  For inspection, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has to show his bank card number to the inspector.
5.  For billing, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives a receipt on each tap for flat fares or at the end of calculation in case of checkin-checkout operating netwrok.
6. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can consult on his mobility service account the taps he did. He can add some "virtual' taps to fill in the list. Each day, a provisional calculation gives the price to paid for the trips of the day. 
7. At the end of the month, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can consult the final price to paid for the month (capping rules). The purchase is done for the amount with the payment method given. 
8. At the end of the year, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER is informed with the automatic renewal of the subscription and with the new tariff applied and the new amount of each month-instalment.
