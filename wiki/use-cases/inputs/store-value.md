**Traveling with a store-value FARE PRODUCT**
--
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchase a membership and units for a mobility service and puts money/tokens/units into a store-value account managed by the service operator. The units are used to purchase and paid for travel. 

Steps : 
0. Optional step : The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER wants to travel with a contactless card, not a de-materialized card embedded in his smartphone : he buys a contactless card in an agency. 
1. With his smartphone, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks for FARE PRODUCTs that are available on the TRANSPORT NETWORKs on which he wants to travel in the CATALOGUE : he wants to use multiple TRANSPORT MODEs on many NETWORKs during the week but also on Sunday.
2. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER chooses a SALES OFFER PACKAGE of AMOUNTOFPRICEUNITS type : he subscribes to the mobility service and purchase some units on the store-value account (amount of money). Moreover, he registred a payment method and thresholds for the automatic-reloading feature. Optional, he manually links the card with the store-value account.
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER travels with his card by tapping on equipment each time it is required : the validation equipment either on entry (flat fare) or on exit (origin-destination fare rule) displays the price paid for the leg. The store-value account is debited by the amount.
4. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives a special message from validation equipment (optional; and smartphone) when automatic reloading is done by the system on his store-value account. 
5. The TRANSPORT CUTOMER can consult each automatic reload, each is a sale.
