**Long-distance bus ride followed by train ride with bike reservation on-board**

The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER books a train ticket and a bus ticket through a RETAILER, both with a bike reservation.

Steps:

1. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER searches for an itinerary from A to B on the RETAILER app or website,  specifies the trip: destination, latest arrival 14:00 tomorrow; declares its needs for a bike spot in the vehicles
2. System retrieves available itineraries and the best one happens to be train + bus, with resulting SALES OFFER PACKAGEs with bike reservation for the two LEGS and respecting a reasonable transfer time between modes, considering he has a bike to carry
3. Two-phase purchase across both providers: long distance bus (OSDM) and rail (OSDM) are confirmed together
4. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives by email (or on the RETAILER app) a TRAVEL DOCUMENT or two TRAVEL DOCUMENTs (barcodes)
5. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER boards the train with the OSDM TRAVEL DOCUMENT in the indicated coach with bike spots
6. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER arrives at train destination and must reach the bus stop within the time requested for changing mode
7. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER boards the bus with the OSDM TRAVEL DOCUMENT

8. Remark 1 : In case of additional bike reservation, it should be returned either as a ancillary offers, bound to mobility services offers, or included in a package (service + bike). Both cases are possible
9. Remark 2 : Concerning step 3 - the API needs to also support mixing OSDM and non-OSDM providers in a two-phase commit. The two-phase commit must be possible across modes and providing systems.
