#### On-street parking session with expiry notification and mid-session extension

The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER parks their private car in an on-street PARKING (TYPE OF PARKING: on street), starts a one-hour session via the MaaS app, and pays using a pre-registered account. At T−10 minutes the platform fires an ACCOUNT AUTO RENEWAL EVENT and delivers a push notification warning that the session is about to expire. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER chooses to extend by 30 minutes; the USAGE VALIDITY PERIOD is extended and an additional PARKING CHARGE BAND is charged immediately. This use case covers the full lifecycle of a pay-by-mobile on-street parking session including proactive time-management and in-session amendment.

**Steps:**
1. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER parks their car and opens the MaaS app; selects the on-street PARKING location (identified by zone code, QR scan at the pay-and-display sign, or GPS snap-to-bay); selects a PARKING BAY and desired duration (1 hour)
2. System presents available PARKING TARIFF options for the selected location and duration; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER selects the 1-hour SALES OFFER PACKAGE; payment is pre-authorised; USAGE VALIDITY PERIOD starts at activation (TYPE OF USAGE TRIGGER: start of session); PARKING RENTAL STATUS is updated to occupied
3. Parking session runs; PARKING RENTAL STATUS reflects the active bay, start time, and scheduled end time; enforcement systems can query bay status in real time
4. At T−10 minutes (50 minutes elapsed) the MaaS platform detects the approaching expiry and generates an ACCOUNT AUTO RENEWAL EVENT; a push notification is delivered to the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER: session ends in 10 minutes, with a one-tap option to extend
5. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER taps "Extend by 30 minutes"; system checks that the PARKING BAY and time slot are available for extension (no maximum-stay restriction hit, no reserved block starting); an additional PARKING CHARGE BAND (30-minute block at the applicable PARKING PRICE) is presented for confirmation
6. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER confirms; extension is purchased; the USAGE VALIDITY PERIOD end time is moved forward by 30 minutes; payment for the extension block is charged immediately to the pre-registered account; TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives a confirmation with the new end time and updated total fare
7. PARKING RENTAL STATUS is updated with the new end time; enforcement systems receive the updated session data; at the new end time the session expires, the PARKING BAY status returns to available, and TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives a final receipt summarising the full session (original + extension duration and combined PARKING PRICE)
