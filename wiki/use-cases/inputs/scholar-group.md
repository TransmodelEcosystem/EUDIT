**Scholar group travel to the museum**
--
The teacher of a school class of 10 years-old children wants to go to the museum by public transport (urban mobility).

Steps : 
1. The teacher looks for a FARE PRODUCT that allows him to travel with 15 children. He finds a group ticket with 2 accompanying adults (COMPANIONs).
2. The teacher (TRANSPORT CUSTOMER) purchases the group ticket for the day of the school trip (15 children, 2 COMPANIONs). He pays by a single direct debit using the the school' SEPA mandate. 
3. the teacher receives an e-mail with a QR code to print.
4. At the metro station entrance, the teacher validates the QR code on the validation equipment 17 times, one traveler passing through the gate each time. 
5. At the exit, the teacher re-validates the QR code to let the children get out the metro station.
