**Urban ride followed by train ride with contactless travel card as a TRAVEL DOCUMENT**

The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER books a bus ticket and train ticket through a RETAILER MaaS application, both must be added on his contacless travel card

Steps:

1. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER searches for an itinerary from A to B on the RETAILER MaaS application, specifies the trip: destination, latest arrival 14:00 tomorrow; declares its travel card as an intended TRAVEL DOCUMENT
2. System retrieves available itineraries and the best one happens to be urban bus + train, with resulting SALES OFFER PACKAGEs for the two LEGS and respecting a reasonable transfer time between modes
3. Two-phase purchase across both providers: urban public transport (InterMoD API) and rail (OSDM) are confirmed together
5. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER sets his travel card on the back of his phone to charge it with both tickets using NFC technology
6. TRANSPORT CUSTOMER boards the urban transport and then the train with his travel card, in the urban transport he can validate on the validation equipments, and in the train he can be checked by the ticket inspector with their CONTROL MEANS device; a CONTROL PASSENGER TRIP EVENT is triggered;
7. 
8. Remark : can be with other TRAVEL DOCUMENTs : mobile ticket (barcode), NFC bus ticket
