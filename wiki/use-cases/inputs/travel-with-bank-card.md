**Traveling with your bank card as a TRAVEL DOCUMENT**
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER purchases and pays each leg of his TRIP by tapping on validation equipment using his bank card during the TRAVEL.

Steps : 
Hypothesis : TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has a valid bank card of a registred bank AUTHORITY allowed on the transport NETWORK.  
1. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER looks for FARE PRODUCTs that are available on the TRANSPORT NETWORKs on which he wants to travel in the CATALOGUE.
2. Among SALES OFFER PACKAGEs, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER checks that there is one compatible with his contactless bank card. 
3. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER gets on and boards a bus by tapping on a validator with his card. The equipment accepts the check-in validation. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives on his smartphone the payment receipt ticket. At a next stop, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER gets off the bus. 
4. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER continues his trip with a metro LEG : he taps when he enters the metro and when he exits the metro. He receives the receipt on exit with the amount actually paid (connexion or not, capping rules, etc...).  
5. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can check on the transport operator web site (dedicated to the users) the amount he paid for the travel.
