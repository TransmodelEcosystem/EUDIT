# 6.5.2.4 Purchase process

## Summary
When a TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has selected one or more PASSENGER FARE
OFFERs and any required choices for a TRAVEL PACKAGE, he/she proceeds to 
the purchase.

## Steps

## Description

It is assumed that the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has chosen and parameterised 
the FARE PRODUCTs and SALES OFFER PACKAGEs presented in a TRAVEL
PACKAGE he/she wants to buy. 
He/she can select at the same time any reduction rights for which he/she is 
eligible, for example, from a rail card, as represented by a SALE DISCOUNT 
RIGHT or USAGE DISCOUNT RIGHT (i.e., other types of FARE PRODUCTs) that 
they already own. 
All the SALES OFFER PACKAGEs are added to his/her "basket".
If on some SALES OFFER PACKAGEs there are mandatory (or optional) 
parameters to select (e.g., luggage constraints, seat class, seat location, etc.,),
the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has to make his selection. For example, the seat 
reservation can be done by selecting a spot on a deck plan (with all 
information about class, window, …) or just with a class selection (reservation 
with quotas), leaving the system to assign a place.  
Once a selection is done, the holding process is started. 
The holding process (locking the resources) lasts a limited time (as a 
parameter), enough to validate and pay for the purchase by the TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMER (some minutes). 
Once the payment is made, the reservation is finalised, and all options and seat 
are definitively reserved. 
In case of unexpected issues arising, a rollback process is possible.
If successful, the traveller receives the evidence of the purchase, reservations 
and TRAVEL DOCUMENTs associated with the SALES OFFER PACKAGEs he has 
bought.
