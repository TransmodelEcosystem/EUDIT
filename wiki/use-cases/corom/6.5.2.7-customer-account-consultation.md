# 6.5.2.7 Customer account consultation
A TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can consult his/her CUSTOMER ACCOUNT and
perform some actions on FARE PRODUCTs-SALES OFFER PACKAGEs he/she
has purchased.

## Steps

## Description

It is assumed that TRANSPORT CUSTOMERs have a CUSTOMER ACCOUNT on a 
mobility platform with at least one contract (and associated CUSTOMER 
PURCHASE PACKAGEs they have purchased).
They can consult their CUSTOMER ACCOUNTs and depending on the platform,
carry out some actions. 
For CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGEs associated with travel already 
completed (or not actually executed), the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can request 
an after-sale action such as a refund (for example, in the case of problems
having occurred during the travel).
For CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGEs associated with future travel, the 
TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can, according to the sales conditions, modify one or 
more elements of the CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGE, modify his travel 
documents, or change the service or certain options. In both cases, the 
customer may change his TRAVEL DOCUMENT and possibly pay an extra 
charge. 
Moreover, for future travel, the reservation (and the trip) can be cancelled
according to the after-sales conditions as specified by USAGE PARAMETERs. 
The access rights are revoked, and the price can be refunded.
At the end of the process, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can consult the 
modifications on TRAVEL PACKAGEs associated with his CUSTOMER 
ACCOUNT.
