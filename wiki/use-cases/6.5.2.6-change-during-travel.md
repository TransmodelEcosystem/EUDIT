## 6.5.2.6 Change during the travel
UC6.1 “Change by the traveller” After-sale request by the traveller.  
After the trip departure, a personal event (e.g., a change of plans, sickness, a forgotten meal, missed connection) can lead the traveller to modify his/her passenger accommodation or to cancel or extend the trip or a part of the trip.  
UC6.2 “Change under the responsibility of any providers involved in the trip (not the traveller)” Disruptions (e.g., accidents) and related trip modifications made by the OPERATOR may modify the trip impacting SALES OFFER PACKAGEs, reservations, final prices, TRAVEL GUARANTEEs and 
TRAVEL DOCUMENTs.

## Steps

## Description
For both use cases, it is assumed that the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER prior to 
travel has made a purchase and also possibly made reservations (through 
requests to a trip planner, purchase of SALES OFFER PACKAGEs, seat 
reservation, etc.), and that the corresponding TRAVEL DOCUMENTs have been
issued. (e.g., paper ticket, printed CB2D on a sheet, smart card, mobile 
application, etc). It is also assumed that the requested action to make a change
is permitted by the terms of sale (as described by USAGE PARAMETERs).
When a traveller starts his travel, he possesses TRAVEL DOCUMENTs which 
provide evidence of his purchased TRAVEL PACKAGEs.


UC6.1 “Change by the traveller”
During travel, travellers may wish to initiate changes to their CUSTOMER 
PURCHASE PACKAGE due to a personal change of plan, missed connections, 
desire for additional services, etc.
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER may consequently request a change: such as 
modification of the trip pattern to cancel or add to parts of the trip (e.g., a 
destination change), or to alter the travel times (as say with a missed 
connection due to the traveller arriving late), or to change the trip options
(e.g., with an additional meal). The traveller can review the new solutions 
using a mobile application or be informed / make arrangements at an 
information desk or ticket office. He can modify the trip pattern, change an 
option or postpone his travel. For each case, the traveller must check his 
CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGE (and its USAGE PARAMETERs describing the 
commercial conditions of sale), TRAVEL DOCUMENTs, and TRAVEL 
GUARANTEEs. He may need to buy new FARE PRODUCTs and possibly be 
refunded for the old ones.
In each case, the USAGE PARAMETERs (EXCHANGING, CANCELLING, 
REFUNDING, etc.) provide information about the conditions of sale governing 
a refund or other change initiated by the user, and whether any additional 
payment is required. The TRAVEL GUARANTEEs associated with the purchase 
may also be relevant for deciding the conditions permitting changes, in 
particular if it was some failure on the part of the operator that led to the
travellers wish for a change.
TRANSPORT CUSTOMER requests for alterations are processed as an aftersale action (purchase) with possible further payment and with evidence of the 
purchase.
UC6.2 “Change under the responsibility of any providers of the trip (i.e.,
not the traveller)”
Coming from a traffic perturbation, further disruptions can impact the 
CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGEs: operator delays (technical, weather, 
strikes, …), un-guaranteed connections, modification of trip options by the 
operator. 
In some circumstances, the OPERATOR may propose a re-accommodation to 
the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER. The proposal can be a substitution of the original
trip with another trip (new schedule, another day, alternative route, etc,) 
or/and a refund when the option is no longer available (e.g., a meal or Wi-Fi
service).
In some cases, the trip or a part of the trip may be cancelled and refunded. 
Then the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER decides to accept one proposal or not and
to request for a compensation for an issue during the travel (for example, a 
broken guaranteed connection, or a very late arrival) according to the TRAVEL 
GUARANTEEs provided with his/her CUSTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGE.
The chosen solution is processed as an after-sale action (purchase) with 
payment and with evidence of the purchase. The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can 
receive new TRAVEL DOCUMENTs, new TRAVEL GUARANTEEs in a new or 
modified CUTOMER PURCHASE PACKAGE
