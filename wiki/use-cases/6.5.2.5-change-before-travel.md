## Change before travel
UC5.1 “After-sale request by the traveller” Once a purchase has been made, a TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can request an after-sale action at any moment during travel, but the focus here is on the process before the travel. 
UC5.2 “Change under the responsibility of any providers involved in the trip for a through ticket (not the traveller)” Change in the previously advertised services (re-accommodation).

## Steps

## Description

For both use cases, it is assumed that the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER has 
prepared his travel. He has purchased a TRAVEL PACKAGE that fits with his 
travel. It can be one or several unit ticket or a complex through ticket with 
guaranteed connections and more than one TRANSPORT MODE (bike and rail, 
rail and urban bus). 
It is also assumed that the requested action is authorised by the terms of sale.
UC5.1 “After-sale request by the traveller”
A TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can decide to change some parameters of his/her
trip before travel according to the commercial conditions placed on the FARE 
PRODUCTs and SALES OFFER PACKAGEs being sold, represented in 
Transmodel by USAGE PARAMETERs such as CANCELLING, REFUNDING, 
EXCHANGING, TRANSFERABILITY, MINIMUM STAY, etc. For all such requests, 
the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must provide evidence of the TRAVEL PACKAGE
he bought (with its associated USAGE PARAMETERs and TRAVEL 
GUARANTEEs) by connecting to his online CUSTOMER ACCOUNT, going to a 
desk, or identifying with his TRAVEL DOCUMENT (anonymous) on a ticket 
vending machine.

For each type of request, a process is starting between both parties in order to 
find an agreement. The after-sales proposals may be different depending on 
the TRAVEL PACKAGE and its specified after-sale conditions and TRAVEL 
GUARANTEEs,, the passenger’s specific choice of options, the moment of the 
request and the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER’s particular wishes. Before the travel
starts, when he/she cancels the travel, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER can 
usually obtain a full or partial refund (depending on the REFUNDING 
parameter)
For a change in reservation or to the TRAVEL DOCUMENTs, usually, the 
TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must pay extra-charges (depending on the 
RESERVING user parameter)
To be noted: During the travel or when it is very close to start, the TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMER can often request for a change in his reservation, take the next train 
or add a step. The after-sale usually proposes a FARE PRODUCT exchange with 
an extra charge. 
After the travel, usual after-sale requests are based on issue during the travel. 
The TRANSPORT CUSTOMER had a delayed train and missed his connection or 
received a fine for an issue with his TRAVEL DOCUMENT. In this case the 
TRANSPORT CUSTOMER must request the after-sale desk for a compensation or 
to pay/not pay the fine.

When the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER request is accepted by the DISTRIBUTOR, 
the after-sale action and payment are executed. 
At the end of the after-sales process, the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER receives the 
evidence, the new/updated TRAVEL PACKAGE and TRAVEL DOCUMENTs.
UC5.2 “Change under the responsibility of any providers involved in the 
trip for a through ticket (not the traveller)”
An OPERATOR can decide schedule changes or experience perturbations to the 
normal schedule (Re-accommodation). 
When such changes occur, the OPERATOR should inform the future travellers 
that their TRAVEL PACKAGEs must be modified (adapted or cancelled). Either 
the OPERATOR contacts the TRANSPORT CUSTOMERs, or the TRANSPORT 
CUSTOMERs take the initiative (if they have been monitoring their travel).
For each type of request, a process is starting between both parties to find an 
agreement.
Usually, the OPERATOR proposes an exchange on another JOURNEY or 
another TRANSPORT MODE for a part of the trip, in case of broken connection, 
or for the full trip in the case of a service cancellation.
If the TRANSPORT CUSTOMER doesn’t accept the exchange or prefers instead 
to have compensation because the proposal doesn’t fit with his/her
expectations (e.g., if the arrival is too late, or travel requires use of another 
transport solution not previously included in the OPERATOR proposal), both 
parties find an agreement as to a suitable compensation. It can be a refund or a 
monetary value to spend on future travel with the same OPERATOR.
